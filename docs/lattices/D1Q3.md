## Quadrature Rule

This lattice exactly integrates polynomials up to the given degree against the Gaussian weight:

\[
\int_{\mathbb{R}^d} f(\boldsymbol{\xi})\, \frac{e^{-|\boldsymbol{\xi}|^2/2}}{(2\pi)^{d/2}} \, d\boldsymbol{\xi} \;=\; \sum_{a} w_a \, f(c\,\boldsymbol{\xi}_a)
\]

where \(d = 1\) and \(c = 1.732050807568877\ldots\)
