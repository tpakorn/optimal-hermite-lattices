\int_{\mathbb{R}^d} f(\boldsymbol{\xi})\, \frac{e^{-|\boldsymbol{\xi}|^2/2}}{(2\pi)^{d/2}} \, d\boldsymbol{\xi} = \sum_{a} w_a \, f(c\,\boldsymbol{\xi}_a)
